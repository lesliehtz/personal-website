---
title: 'Tones of Morocco'
date: '2023'
description: "This series explores the arid Moroccan countryside, focusing on the colors and textures that shape the landscape and the objects within it. Earth tones dominate, with occasional unexpected greens or man-made elements, all beneath an endless blue sky. Of all these colors, orange has always been my favorite, so I naturally find myself drawn to it wherever it appears. This work is both a documentation of place and a personal pursuit of a color I hold close."
